About the app

Think you know your friends? Prove it. Each round, one player ranks items according to the prompt, and everyone else tries to predict their order. Online multiplayer or pass-and-play on one device, for 2–6 players.

Play together, anywhere

Online multiplayer or pass-and-play on a single device. No sign-up needed.

Rank and predict

One player orders the items, everyone else guesses how they did it.

Co-op or Competitive

Team up or try to beat your friends across different game modes.
