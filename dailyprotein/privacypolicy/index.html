Privacy policy

Daily Protein privacy policy

Last updated September 2026Applies to Daily Protein for iOS

How EwanX handles information in the Daily Protein app. In short: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and nothing that identifies you.

What we collect

Nothing. Daily Protein never asks for your name, email address, phone number or location, and there is no way for us to tell who you are. Your food log, targets and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted along with the app.

The app does not send usage data of any kind. There is no advertising in Daily Protein, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Camera and food lookups

If you scan a barcode, the camera is used only to read the code on the packet. Nothing it sees is stored or sent anywhere. The app then looks up that barcode in a public food database to fetch the nutrition figures. The request contains the barcode and nothing else about you or your device.

Who we share it with

No one. Daily Protein uses no analytics, advertising networks or social media integrations.

If you buy the in-app purchase, payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card details, billing address or Apple ID.

Children

Because nothing we collect identifies anyone, we hold no personal information about any user, children included.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and everything stored on your device goes with it. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete.

Changes and contact

Any updates to this policy appear on this page with a new date.
